# halftime-performance-change

# Introduction
From the 2026 MLS season match data upto the penultimate matchday, we can compute a club's non-penalty xG difference (that is, the difference between their npxG generated and npxG conceded), not only for an entire match but also for each of their halves. npxG difference is a useful stat that can roughly tell us how well a team generally performs, with both offensive and defensive ability being crucial for increasing the stat. Thus comparing a club's average npxG difference between their first and second halves can tell us how a club's performance might change in between the break.

# Observations

Philadelphia Union is a prime example of a second-half dominator. They generate the second highest non-penalty xG difference (i.e., npxG for minus npxG against) at 0.45. However, an overwhelming portion of this stat is generated in the second half. The club averages 0.02 non-penalty xG difference in the first half, and 0.43 npxG difference in the second half.

RB New York is the prime example of a team of two halves. They generate about 0.34 npxG difference in the first half and -0.33 npxG difference in the second half, a difference of 0.68 npxG difference. They have the highest change in npxG difference across the two halves in the entire league.
